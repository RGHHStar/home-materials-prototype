What makes a useful description?

  • Colour: yellow, pink, or grey
  • Form: fluffy, foam, granules, or rigid board
  • Texture: soft, hard, or crumbly
  • Location: attic, wall cavity, or basement

Do not touch or remove an unknown material with bare hands. Suspicious materials in older homes should be inspected by a professional.
